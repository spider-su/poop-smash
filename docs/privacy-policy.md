# Poop Smash Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 7 November 2026

**App:** Poop Smash (`com.spidersu.poopsmash`)

**Publisher:** Alex Kotik (individual). **Google Play developer name:** smart-box.

**Contact:** alex.kotik.net@gmail.com

## Data handled
Poop Smash is an offline single-player cartoon game. It does not require an account and its current Android version does not include advertising, analytics, cloud saves, in-app purchases, tracking SDKs, or developer-operated online services.

The app stores only local gameplay information such as best scores, plus sound and vibration preferences, on the user's device. This information is used to remember progress and preferences, and is not transmitted to the developer.

The current game does not access contacts, location, camera, microphone, photographs or advertising IDs, and does not collect names or email addresses through gameplay. It does not request Android internet permission. Optional vibration uses Android's vibration permission. No personal information is sold or shared with third parties through the app.

## Retention and deletion
Locally stored information persists until the player clears Android app storage or uninstalls the app, subject to the operating system's normal backup and restore behavior. The developer cannot access or delete this data remotely. To erase local data, open Android Settings, find Poop Smash, and clear storage, or uninstall it.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Its intended age group is declared separately in Google Play Console.

## Security
The app does not transmit personal data to a developer-operated server. It relies on Android's application storage isolation for its local information.

## Changes
If later versions add networking, analytics, ads, accounts, or additional data handling, we will update this policy and the Play Console data disclosures before that release.

## Questions
Email **alex.kotik.net@gmail.com**. This privacy policy applies to Poop Smash, not to Google's separate processing of information through Google Play.
