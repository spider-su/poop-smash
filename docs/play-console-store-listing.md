## Full description
Squish it. Stretch it. Smash it!

Meet the wobbliest cartoon characters around in Poop Smash, a silly touch-based game full of funny reactions, playful sounds and spectacular splats.

Touch and hold to squash your character, drag to stretch it, and keep going until it bursts into colorful goo.

Play your way:
• Free Play: Experiment with all the squishy interactions at your own pace.
• 60-Second Challenge: Smash characters, build combos and try to set a new personal best.

Meet four goofy characters:
• Classic: A friendly and bouncy original.
• Frozen: An icy character that's tougher to squash.
• Golden: Earn bonus points.
• Volcano: Ready for an explosive reaction.

Enjoy expressive faces, humorous sounds, bright visuals and satisfying touch controls.

Poop Smash works offline. Your best score and sound and vibration settings are stored locally on your device.

The current version has no accounts, advertisements or in-app purchases.
